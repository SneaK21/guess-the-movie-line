Movie Script Challenge

Guess the line, word by word

How to play: Pick a film above. Each passage describes a moment from the film in brackets. Below it, the actual line is hidden behind asterisks. Click any hidden word to guess just that word (you can also spend a few points on a letter hint if you're stuck), or click anywhere else on the line to target it, then type the words that are still hidden and press Enter to check. Correct words lock in and stay revealed forever; incorrect or unmatched words stay hidden. You don't need to retype words already revealed. Each correct word is worth +1 point; nail an entire line correctly on your very first attempt for some little extra bonus points. Good luck and have fun remembering your favorite lines!
