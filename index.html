Movie Script Challenge

Guess the line, word by word

How to play: Pick a film above. Each passage describes a moment from the film in brackets. Below it, the actual line is hidden behind asterisks. Click any hidden word to guess just that word (you can also spend a point on a letter hint if you're stuck), or click anywhere else on the line to target it, then type any words you remember — in any order, not just from the start — and press Enter to check. Correct words lock in and stay revealed forever, wherever they fall in the line; incorrect words are just ignored. Each correct word is worth +1 point; nail an entire line correctly on your very first attempt for some little extra bonus points. Good luck and have fun remembering your favorite lines!
